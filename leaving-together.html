Bye Bye Meta

Would you
leave Meta?

If a reliable local alternative existed and other Brussels institutions moved there too, would you leave Facebook and Instagram?

The challenge is not simply whether cultural organisations want to leave Meta, but what makes leaving possible.

Of the 26 responses, 25 use Instagram and 23 use Facebook. For a local alternative, 6 use Mastodon and 1 uses Bluesky.

What the survey shows

  1. Leaving is conditional, not refused. 21 of 26 responses are open to leaving. 5 said no.
  2. The alternatives already exist. Newsletters 23, cultural listings 22 and print 20 of 26.
  3. Staying isn’t about paid reach. 11 of the 17 respondents who allowed their ad answers to be counted never pay for ads.
  4. Silence is also data. About 60 of the 85 organisations contacted did not reply.

One mark per survey response

    85 organisations were asked. 26 responses came back. About 60 stayed silent, and silence is data too.
